Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Application purpose

Rclone RaspberryPi500 is a personal, non-commercial application used by its owner to access and manage files in their own Google Drive account using rclone.

Google user data

The application requests Google Drive access to read, create, update and delete files as directed by the account owner. This may include an encrypted KeePass password database.

Storage and security

Google OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner's Raspberry Pi. Files may be cached locally by rclone. Access to these files is controlled by the owner.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold or shared with advertisers or other third parties. Data is transferred between the owner's device and Google Drive as required for the application's functionality.

Data retention and deletion

Local cached files and OAuth credentials can be deleted by the owner. Google Drive files remain under the owner's control.

Contact

This application is intended solely for personal use and is not available to external users.
